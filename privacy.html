Privacy Policy

Alza Mailbot · Effective 28 September 2026

Alza Mailbot ("the service") is a demo email assistant that automatically answers messages sent to one dedicated mailbox, techmailbot6@gmail.com. This policy describes what data the service touches and how it is handled.

What data the service accesses

The service uses the Gmail API to access only its own dedicated mailbox. It never accesses any other Google account or mailbox. For messages arriving in that mailbox it reads the sender address, subject, message text and attachments, sends replies, and marks processed messages as read.

How the data is used

Incoming message content is used for a single purpose: generating a reply. The message text, conversation history of the same email thread and attachments are passed to Google Vertex AI (Gemini) to produce the answer, which is then sent back to the sender. The service does not use message content for advertising, profiling, or training of models, and does not sell or share it with any third party.

What is stored

The service keeps a minimal processing state in Google Cloud Firestore (region europe-west3, EU): Gmail message identifiers and a mailbox history position, used to avoid answering the same email twice. Message content and attachments are processed in memory only and are not persisted by the service; the emails themselves remain in the Gmail mailbox under Google's own terms.

Limited Use disclosure

Alza Mailbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

If you emailed the mailbox and want your messages or the related processing records deleted, write to techmailbot6@gmail.com and they will be removed.
